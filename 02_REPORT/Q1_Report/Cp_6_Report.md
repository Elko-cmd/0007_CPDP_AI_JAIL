## 6. Title vs. content — structural note (carried forward)
"A Voice From Inside" and the TLDR promise someone who has been inside the system. The content delivers second-person narration *about* and *to* that person ("You were asked 137 questions") — the narrator speaks to the subject, not from the subject's position. The voice from inside never actually speaks in first person.

This is a design choice, not an error. The second-person form is effective — it places the listener in the position of the processed subject. But the title and TLDR promise a first-person voice that the content doesn't deliver.

## 7. "Profiles do not expire" — ending note
The chapter closes with "You thought it was a questionnaire. It was a profile. Profiles do not expire." This ends in the head (a thesis) rather than the body (a sensation). The blueprint recommends ending in the body. Consider: the form in the hands, the room, the door closing — a physical detail after the insight.
